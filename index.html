100+dishes on the menu

From a quick bacon roll on the way to work to a long, lazy brunch with the family, we cook everything fresh to order and serve it with a smile. Our menu runs from classic Scottish and English breakfasts to toasties, paninis, baked tatties and grills — there's something on it for everyone.

Bring the kids (there's a menu just for them), bring the dog, or just bring yourself. Walk in, or give us a ring and we'll keep a table for you.
